PERSONAL MAILBOX UTILITY

Terms of use

Gmail Mailbox Organization is a personal mailbox utility operated by its account owner. This website provides information about the utility; it does not provide a hosted email-management service or accept registrations.

Authorization and use

Use is limited to Gmail accounts whose owners have authorized the script. The account owner reviews the configuration, grants Google permissions, reviews the cleanup preview, and approves the recurring cleanup policy before enabling its trigger.

Behavior and limitations

The utility is intended to label matching mail and move eligible promotional and newsletter conversations older than 90 days to Gmail Trash. Protected-message checks and bounded batches can leave older messages in the inbox. Automated classification is not a guarantee that every message is categorized correctly. The owner should review results and use the Keep label or disable the trigger when necessary.

The current owner-approved policy moves eligible mail to Trash, where messages remain recoverable for up to 30 days before Gmail automatically permanently deletes them. The script does not directly permanently delete messages, empty Trash, mark messages as read, or send email. A temporary two-hour backlog schedule stops after one complete scan or seven days.

Availability and changes

Operation depends on Google authorization, Apps Script execution limits, and Gmail API availability. There is no service-level commitment or paid subscription. The account owner may change or stop the utility at any time.

Privacy and questions

Use of Google account data is described in the privacy policy. Questions can be directed through the maintainer’s GitHub profile.
