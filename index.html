PERSONAL MAILBOX UTILITY

Gmail Mailbox Organization

A personal Google Apps Script utility that organizes one authorized Gmail account and archives selected older promotional mail and newsletters.

How it works

The account owner authorizes the script through Google. Label-only Gmail filters organize matching messages. An optional daily Apps Script trigger checks eligible inbox conversations whose latest activity is older than 90 days.

  • Archives by removing the Inbox label; messages remain in All Mail.
  • Does not delete messages, mark messages as read, or send email.
  • Excludes starred and important messages, sent or draft conversations, personal-category mail, recent activity, and detected financial, receipt, security, and transactional content.
  • Organizes TS2068 mailing-list mail separately and excludes it from recurring archiving.
  • Protects custom labels other than Reading, including Keep and Review/Unreadable.
  • Holds messages it cannot safely interpret for review.

Owner-controlled automation

Archiving is disabled until the account owner approves the policy after a preview and successful tests. Daily runs process bounded batches, so a backlog can remain visible beyond 90 days. Classification is conservative and may leave promotional mail in the inbox.

This is a personal utility, not a public sign-up service. No Google login or credentials are collected by this website. Read the privacy policy and terms of use.
